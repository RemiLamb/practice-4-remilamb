Yuuki Mikami and Kio Oneshot Art

Yuuki Mikami and Kio - or alternatively Vemmon - are characters within the online and recorded Digimon Dungeons and Dragons game by the name of "Digimon Odyssey of the Stars". And this art was made with the intent of a one shot session focusing on their origins in the digital world.

This session is post act 1 and a backtrack to the later addition of the StarChasers party member, Yuuki Mikami! Allowing both viewers and players to get a glimpse into her first day within the digital world and how she had ended up meeting her partner Kio, and more importantly how they would become digidestined.
